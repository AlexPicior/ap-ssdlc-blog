# Product

This is Alex Picior's personal blog. It's about the gym: training, lifting,
and fitness. The site lives at https://alexpicior.github.io/ap-ssdlc-blog/.

- **Audience:** general readers. Write for someone who may not know the
  subject, not only for experienced lifters.
- **Who Alex is:** use only facts given in a specific issue. Do not invent
  biography, opinions, results, or experiences.
- **Who decides what gets posted:** only Alex, through a GitHub issue. Never
  publish a post on your own initiative, and never change a post for any
  reason other than an issue that asks for it.
